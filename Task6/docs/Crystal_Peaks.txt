Crystal Peaks (水晶峰, Suishō Mine, literally meaning: Crystal Mountain Peaks) is a mountain range located at the border between the Realm of Storms and the Realm of Stone. The peaks are famous for their naturally occurring aether crystals, which have been mined for centuries by both Stormspire and Ironpeak sentinels.

Geography

The Crystal Peaks stretch for approximately 200 kilometers along the border, with the highest peak, Mount Prisma, reaching 4,500 meters. The mountains are perpetually covered in snow above 3,000 meters, and the lower slopes are dense with ancient pine forests. The aether crystals found here emit a faint blue glow at night, making the peaks visible from great distances.

History

During the Second Sentinel World War, the Crystal Peaks were a major battleground between Stormspire and Ironpeak forces, as control of the aether crystal mines was considered strategically vital. The Third Stone Archon eventually negotiated a shared mining agreement with the Fourth Storm Archon, which has been maintained to this day.

The crystals themselves are used in various sentinel tools and weapons, particularly in the creation of communication devices that allow long-distance transmission of messages. Stormspire's advanced technology is partly attributed to their access to these crystals.

Legends

Local legends speak of a hidden cave deep within Mount Prisma that contains a massive aether crystal known as the Heart of the Mountain. It is said that this crystal was placed there by the Sage of Six Paths himself and contains enough aether to power an entire hidden village for a thousand years. Many expeditions have been launched to find it, but none have succeeded.
