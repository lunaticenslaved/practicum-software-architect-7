Shadowfen (影沼の里, Shadowfen no Sato, literally meaning: Village Hidden in Shadow Marshes) is a minor hidden village located in the swamplands south of the Realm of Embers. Unlike the Five Great Sentinel Countries, Shadowfen has never had an Archon and is instead governed by a council of elder sentinels known as the Marsh Wardens.

History

Shadowfen was founded during the Warring States Period by a group of rogue sentinels who fled the conflicts between the major clans. They discovered a network of caves beneath the marshlands that provided natural protection from invaders. The village remained neutral during most major conflicts, including the Great Convergence War, though it secretly provided medical supplies to the Allied Sentinel Forces.

The village is known for its unique Water and Earth Release combination techniques, which allow its sentinels to manipulate the swamp terrain to their advantage. Shadowfen sentinels are also renowned herbalists, as the marshlands contain rare medicinal plants not found elsewhere.

Notable Residents

Marsh Warden Yuki — Current head of the council, known for her mastery of Poison Mist techniques.
Kaito the Healer — A legendary medical sentinel who developed cures for several primal beast-related ailments.
Ren Bogwalker — A scout sentinel famous for navigating the treacherous marshlands blindfolded.

Relations

Shadowfen maintains trade relations with Verdania and Misthollow, exchanging rare herbs for weapons and supplies. Despite its small size, the village is respected for its neutrality and medical expertise.
