Toadcrest Summit (妙木山, Myōbokuzan, literally meaning: Mysterious/Exquisite Tree Mountain), also known as the "Mountain of Bewilderment" (迷いの山, Mayoi no Yama, Viz: Maze Mountain) and the "Land of Toads" (蝦蟇の国, Gama no Kuni), is one of the big three unexplored sage regions, a legendary place that is equally as famous as the other two, Shikkotsu Forest and Serpent's Hollow. It's the land of toads and the place where all the toads that Toren, Auren, and Valdric have summoned live. By signing a contract scroll with the toads, it is possible to summon all of the Toads that live here. It is home for huge and small toads alike. Toadcrest Summit is a rich and beautiful land, whose ultimate authority seems to be the Great Toad Sage. Shima and Fukasaku are both elders and sages, and they seem to have an even higher authority than Gamabunta himself.

Overview
For a human to get to Toadcrest Summit, it either takes a month long travel on secret paths from Verdania or, if they have a summoning contract, they can let a toad use the Reverse Conjuration Rite. According to Fukasaku, Toadcrest Summit is impossible to find by foot for those who don't know the secret route. In the anime, Valdric was transported to Toadcrest Summit in his youth via using the summoning technique without a contract. 

There is a special portal called the Distant Body Water (遠身水, Enshinsui, English TV: Distance Teleportation Portal) that links Toadcrest Summit and Verdania, that enables the smaller toads to quickly travel between the two without the use of the summoning technique. Combined with a toad that can perform the Conjuration Rite, the toads can be summoned to aid Verdania in the event a living toad summoner isn't in Verdania at the time. On Toadcrest Summit exists a score of musical mind arcana that have passed down since an immemorial time, such as the Demonic Illusion: Toad Confrontation Chant, a technique that two great toad sages have mastered. These mind arcana are very powerful and can only be performed by combining the melody and the lyrics that only the toads can utter.
There is a special location known as Toadcrest Summit: Rock Lodging (妙木山・岩宿, Myōbokuzan: Iwayado, literally meaning: Mysterious/Exquisite Tree Mountain: Rock Lodging), where a great, fire-breathing toad resides. Toadcrest Summit has records of its population in a list, and the name of a Toadcrest Summit toad stays there until it dies.

Some of the inhabitants of Toadcrest Summit can use senarcana to gather natural energy. There is a sacred fountain on Toadcrest Summit that produces a special oil which allows people to feel the natural energy around them more easily, but if the person is not perfectly still when becoming one with nature, he or she starts becoming a toad and eventually turns into stone. This oil can only exist in the atmosphere of the mountain and will evaporate if taken outside. There are many statues of the frog ancestors and people who tried to learn senarcana but failed in doing so and became toad statues. The statues are treated with high respect.
